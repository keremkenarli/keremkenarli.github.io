About

I am a gastroenterologist with a particular interest in advanced therapeutic endoscopy, including ERCP, endoscopic ultrasound (EUS), pancreatobiliary diseases, inflammatory bowel disease, and clinical research. My academic work focuses on improving endoscopic management strategies and producing clinically relevant research in gastrointestinal diseases.

Research Interests

Hepatobiliary Diseases

Clinical Research

Retrospective cohort studies, endoscopic outcomes and evidence-based medicine.
