Biography

Dr. Kerem Kenarlı is a gastroenterologist currently practicing at Ankara Bilkent City Hospital, Türkiye.

Born in Konya in 1990, Dr. Kenarlı graduated from Meram Science High School in 2008. He received his medical degree from Hacettepe University Faculty of Medicine in 2015. He completed his residency in Internal Medicine at Ankara University Faculty of Medicine in 2021, followed by fellowship training in Gastroenterology at Ankara Bilkent City Hospital, which he completed in 2026.

He fulfilled his compulsory medical service in Tosya State Hospital, Kastamonu between 2015 and 2016, and later in Bingöl between 2021 and 2023.

Since completing his gastroenterology training, he has been working as a gastroenterologist at Ankara Bilkent City Hospital.
