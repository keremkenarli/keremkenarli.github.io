Research Interests

My research focuses primarily on advanced pancreatobiliary endoscopy and clinical outcomes of complex endoscopic interventions.

Therapeutic ERCP & Complex Biliary Interventions

Advanced ERCP techniques and endoscopic management of complex biliary strictures and difficult biliary access.

Post-Liver Transplant Biliary Complications

Endoscopic management and long-term outcomes of biliary complications following liver transplantation.

Bile Duct Injuries

Endoscopic and multidisciplinary management of complex bile duct injuries and biliary anatomical variations.

Pancreatic Fluid Collections & Walled-Off Necrosis

EUS-guided drainage, direct endoscopic necrosectomy, and treatment strategies for pancreatic fluid collections.

Inflammatory Bowel Disease

Clinical outcomes, treatment response, and complications in patients with inflammatory bowel disease.
